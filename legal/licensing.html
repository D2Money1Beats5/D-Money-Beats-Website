2. What a non-exclusive license allows

Unless the order-specific license says otherwise, a paid non-exclusive license lets the named purchaser create and commercially release one new song using the selected beat. The purchaser may record vocals or other original elements over the beat and distribute the resulting song through normal streaming, download, video, social, radio, live-performance and promotional channels within the usage limits attached to the purchased tier.

3. What is not transferred

A non-exclusive purchase does not transfer ownership of the underlying beat, producer composition, sound recording, project files, trademarks, or producer publishing interest. D-Money Beats may continue to license the same beat to other customers. A purchaser may not resell, re-license, upload, give away, or distribute the raw beat or stems by themselves.

4. Credit

Where platform fields permit, credit the producer as “Prod. by D-Money” or substantially similar wording.

5. Content ID and automated claims

Customers may not register the raw beat or a derivative song in YouTube Content ID, Meta Rights Manager, TikTok Rights Manager, or another automated fingerprinting/claim system without written permission. D-Money Beats may administer Content ID for its catalog. A properly licensed customer may request claim review or whitelisting for an authorized release, subject to verification of the order and license.

6. Samples and third-party material

If a beat contains disclosed third-party samples, loops, or licensed source material, the order-specific license or beat notes will identify any material restrictions known to D-Money Beats. The artist remains responsible for clearance of any third-party material the artist adds to the new song.

7. Exclusive inquiries

An exclusive sale is not complete until both parties sign a separate written agreement and required payment clears. Pre-existing valid non-exclusive licenses survive unless the exclusive agreement expressly provides otherwise. Publishing, copyright, master ownership, royalty splits, and buyout terms are transferred only to the extent stated in the signed agreement.

8. Acceptance and records

Electronic acceptance at checkout, the order receipt, beat title, tier, date, purchaser identity, and license version together form the purchase record. Keep your receipt and license copy.
