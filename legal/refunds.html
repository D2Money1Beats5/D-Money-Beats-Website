Digital Delivery & Refund Policy

Effective date: September 12, 2026.

Beat-license delivery

After successful payment, the checkout record should generate a receipt, the purchased license, and access to the file formats included in the selected tier. Public site previews are not the purchased delivery files. Keep the receipt and license for your records.

Service delivery

Mixing/mastering customers receive an order handoff for source-file submission and project notes. Final delivery depends on receipt of usable files and the agreed scope.

Refunds for digital goods

Because downloadable beat files and license rights can be delivered immediately, completed digital-license purchases are generally final once access or delivery has been provided. This does not limit any non-waivable consumer right. D-Money Beats may issue a refund or replacement for verified duplicate charges, an incorrect file delivered due to store error, a materially defective download that cannot be corrected, or another situation where law requires a remedy.

Service cancellations

If a mixing/mastering project is cancelled before work begins, a refund may be available after payment-processing costs or non-refundable setup items where permitted by law. Once work has begun, the refund, credit, or revision remedy depends on the amount of work completed and the confirmed project scope.

Chargebacks

Contact support first if an order is missing, duplicated, or incorrect. Filing a fraudulent chargeback after receiving licensed files may result in suspension of download access and preservation of the order/license record for dispute response.

Delivery problems

If delivery does not arrive, use the site contact form with the purchase email, order date, and beat/service name. Do not include full card information.
