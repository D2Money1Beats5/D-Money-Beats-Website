Information may be processed by vendors used to host the site, process payments, deliver email, store protected files, analyze performance, or prevent abuse.

Retention

Order and licensing records may be retained as reasonably necessary to document permissions, support customers, comply with tax/accounting obligations, resolve disputes, and protect legal rights. Marketing data may be removed or suppressed after an unsubscribe request.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the unsubscribe method in those messages. You may contact D-Money Beats through the website regarding access, correction, or deletion requests, subject to records that must be retained for legitimate legal or business reasons.

Security

Reasonable safeguards are used, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security. Do not submit sensitive information through general contact forms unless requested through a secure order or upload flow.

Children

This store is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
